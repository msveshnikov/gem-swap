**Full description:**

Swap, match and sparkle! Gem Swap is a brand-new match-3 puzzle with real faceted gems that catch the light, juicy explosions and six magical worlds.

Swipe a gem to swap it with a neighbour and line up three or more of a kind. Watch them shatter, let new gems fall in and chain cascades for big combos!

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
💎 8 beautifully cut gems — ruby, fire topaz, citrine, emerald, sapphire, amethyst, star diamond and rose heart — each with its own shape and glint
⚡ Special gems: match 4 for a Line gem, an L or T for a Bomb, 5 in a row for a rainbow Prism
💥 Swap two specials together for giant combos: crosses, mega-blasts and a Prism that wipes the whole board
🧊 Break ice, smash stone blocks and collect gems across 60 levels — then endless bonus levels
🌍 Six animated worlds: Crystal Caves, Sunken Temple, Golden Dunes, Firefly Forest, Fire Forge and Star Crown
⏱ Blitz mode: 90 seconds, as many points as you can!
🎆 Leftover moves turn into a fireworks bonus at the end of every level
